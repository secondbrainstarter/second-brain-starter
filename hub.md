# Second Brain Starter — Free Obsidian Mini-Vault

Three folders, three templates, three rules — and you have a place for everything
that crosses your mind, plus a daily routine that costs you 17 minutes a day.

This is the free entry point to the **Second Brain Starter**: a complete,
ready-to-use Obsidian vault with templates in English **and** German.

## What's inside

| Folder | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| `00_Inbox` | Inbox — everything lands here, emptied in the evening |
| `06_Daily` | Daily notes & journal |
| `07_Templates` | 6 templates (3 EN + 3 DE): daily note, project note, permanent note |

No community plugins required — it runs on stock Obsidian.

## The three rules (the whole trick)

1. **Capture, don't sort.** Everything lands in `00_Inbox/` first — sorting
   happens in the evening (5 minutes).
2. **One note, one idea.** Write in your own words what *you* mean.
3. **Always link.** Every note links at least one other note — that turns a
   collection into a network, and the network is the "second brain".

## Quick start (10 minutes)

1. Download Obsidian for free from https://obsidian.md
2. Open this repo's `second-brain-in-10-minuten-v0.2.zip` (or clone the repo)
   and open the folder as a vault in Obsidian.
3. Create today's daily note from the template (`07_Templates/Daily-Note.md` or
   `Tagesnote.md`) in `06_Daily/` — filename = today's date.
4. From tomorrow: 7 minutes in the morning (daily note + 3 goals), 10 minutes
   in the evening (process inbox, close the daily note).
